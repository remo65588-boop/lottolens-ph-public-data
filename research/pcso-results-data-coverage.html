DATA METHODOLOGY

PCSO draw data coverage and methodology

This note explains what the LottoLens PH open dataset covers, where the schedule facts come from, how fields are normalized, and what the data should not be used to claim.

Dataset scope

The dataset describes the normal draw schedule for 2D Lotto, 3D Lotto, 4D Lotto, 6D Lotto, Lotto 6/42, Mega Lotto 6/45, Super Lotto 6/49, Grand Lotto 6/55, and Ultra Lotto 6/58. It records normal draw days, draw times, result formats, number ranges, and plain-language field definitions.

It is intentionally small. Live winning-number rows remain in the maintained LottoLens PH historical draw archive so that schedule metadata and frequently changing results are not confused.

Sources and provenance

Schedule facts are compiled from public Philippine Charity Sweepstakes Office game information and draw-schedule materials. The official PCSO website remains the final authority for holiday suspensions, special draws, schedule changes, and official result announcements.

Each published field is defined in a separate CSV. The repository also includes Frictionless Data metadata, machine-readable table schemas, citation metadata, and a public license so researchers can inspect the package without reverse-engineering the website.

Normalization rules

  • All times are represented in Philippine Time (PHT, UTC+8).
  • Game names use a stable canonical label across files.
  • Digit-result lengths and jackpot number ranges are stored explicitly.
  • Leading zeroes and published number order should be preserved in result archives.
  • Unknown or unavailable facts should remain empty rather than estimated.

Update policy

The schedule package is reviewed when PCSO announces a material change. Live result pages are updated separately after draw windows because a schedule record and a verified winning result have different update lifecycles. The result-verification guide describes how to match the game, date, session, number order, and source.

Limitations

A normal schedule is not a guarantee that a draw will occur at that time. Public holidays, official suspensions, technical delays, or special announcements can change operations. Independent archives can also lag behind an official correction, so new or disputed results should be checked against an official PCSO source.

Historical frequency, hot and cold numbers, and missing streaks are descriptive summaries. They do not predict a random future draw and do not support guaranteed-win claims. LottoLens PH is independent and is not affiliated with or endorsed by PCSO.
