# LottoLens PH Public Data Community Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want participation in the LottoLens PH public-data project to be safe,
respectful, and useful. Contributors, reviewers, and maintainers are expected
to treat one another with patience and professional courtesy regardless of
background, identity, experience level, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss the data, sources, and proposed changes rather than attacking people.
- Support factual claims with reproducible evidence or a public source.
- Respect privacy and do not publish personal or confidential information.
- Accept correction, explain disagreements constructively, and help newcomers.
- Keep project spaces focused on public information, research, and verification.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized conduct, deliberate
misinformation, spam, doxxing, and sustained disruption are not permitted in
issues, pull requests, discussions, commits, or other project spaces.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to `helloloombase@gmail.com`. Include links,
dates, and enough context for the maintainers to review the incident. Reports
will be handled as confidentially as practical. Maintainers may edit or remove
content, issue a warning, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently
ban a participant when necessary to protect the project community.

Maintainers will avoid conflicts of interest and will document enforcement
decisions internally. Retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good
faith is prohibited.

## Scope

This code applies in all project-managed spaces and when a participant publicly
represents the project.
